Create cards for the lesson's definitions and properties.
- A key word to be able to define: one card. Front: the key word. Back: its definition, short, as the lesson gives it. Info: a short example, if the lesson gives one.
- A property or a theorem, in a box or not (any sentence of the lesson stating one): two cards. One, front: its name (e.g. “Pythagoras' theorem”); back: the full statement, all its hypotheses then its conclusion (“If …, then …”). The other, front: “If … (all the hypotheses), then…?”; back: the conclusion. Info: what it is for (e.g. “to work out a length”).
- Its converse, when the lesson gives it: two cards of its own, the same way (it is not the reverse card).
- What the lesson says about its key figures (a configuration, what it shows): one card, with the figure.
