Create dictation cards from the lesson.
For each word to learn to spell: Front: a short sentence using the word, with the word replaced by "…". Back: the word alone, written exactly as in the sentence.
For a text to learn to write (this week's dictation): one card per sentence, and for a long sentence, one card per meaningful group of words. Front: "Sentence 1", "Sentence 2"… in the text's order. Back: the sentence or group of words, written exactly as in the text, with its punctuation.
Info: a useful spelling tip (silent letter, word family…), otherwise empty.
Without a photo: from what I write here (a list of words, or a text).
