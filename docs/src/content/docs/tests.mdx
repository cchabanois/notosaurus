A test on Monday covers three lessons? A **test to prepare** gathers them, whole or some of
their cards only, so that the child revises them together, whatever their decks. Once revised,
the cards go back to their decks.

## Making a test

- From a lesson: **📝 Prepare for a test…**, in its review, adds it to a test (a name, a date),
  whole or **some of its cards only** (a tick on each card). Once the lesson is in a test, the
  button shows the test's name (**📌 …**).
- From the list: the tests are at the top of **My lessons**, where **＋ New test** makes one. Its
  sheet then adds lessons (**＋ Add lessons**, whole) and sends to Anki those not there yet.

![A test to prepare: its lessons, and how to revise it in Anki](../../assets/screenshots/en/exam.png)

## In Anki: a tag

Every card of a test gets a tag in Anki, `Exam::<name>`. It is put on its notes right away when
they are in Anki already, or with them when the lesson is sent.

Deleting a test (**Delete the test**, in its sheet) takes the tag off. The cards stay in Anki,
with their review history.

## Revising it

The test's sheet says how, step by step. With Anki's **custom study**, by hand:

1. Open the deck holding the lessons.
2. **Custom Study** → **Study by card state or tag**, then **All cards in random order (don't
   reschedule)**.
3. Tick the tag `Exam::<name>` and confirm.

Anki makes a session with these cards; once done, they go back to their decks. When the
lessons are in different decks, the sheet gives a search to paste into **Tools → Create Filtered Deck**,
with a **Copy** button.

A filtered deck gathers them faster:

1. In the test's sheet, **Open in AnkiDroid**: AnkiDroid's browser opens with the test's
   cards, whatever their decks.
2. Tap **⋮**, then **Create filtered deck**, even if the list looks empty.
3. Tap **Build**. Over 100 cards, raise **Limit to** first.

AnkiDroid lets no other app make a filtered deck itself: these three taps are needed. See
[The app and AnkiDroid](../app-ankidroid/).

With Anki open, **Revise now in Anki** does it in one click: a filtered deck "Test: …" with the
test's cards, opened in Anki. It is made again each time, and deleted with the test.
