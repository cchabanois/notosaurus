On this phone (**📱 On this phone**), the app works with a subscription, **by the month or by the
year**, paid through Google Play. It pays for what the Notosaurus service does: the AI and the
voices. The prices are shown in the app, as Google Play gives them.

Without a subscription, the app is free with the Anki add-on on your computer: see
[With your computer](../app-with-computer/).

## The free trial

**🎁 Your free trial, no card, will start with your first lesson.** Nothing to tap: the first
lesson you make starts it, and the app then says how many credits it gives and until when.

- **No card** to give, and nothing to cancel: at its end, the app asks you to subscribe to go on.
- **Once per phone.** Google checks that it is the app installed from Google Play, on a real
  phone (Play Integrity), and the app knows this phone by a fingerprint, never its identifier
  itself. On an emulator, the trial isn't offered.

![The app's settings without a subscription: the free trial will start with the first lesson](../../assets/screenshots/en/app-settings.png)

## Subscribing

In the settings, **🔑 Subscription** offers the subscription by the month and by the year, with
their price. Google Play takes the payment; once it is done, the app says **✓ Subscribed: happy
revising!**

**🔑 Subscription** then shows the plan, until when it is paid, and when it is renewed.

## Credits

The subscription gives **credits each month**. **🔑 Subscription** shows how many are left this
month and today, and when they are renewed.

What the service does for you uses them: making and correcting the cards, explaining one, the
voices, the pictures. Before drawing a picture, the app says about how many credits it will cost.
When there are none left, they come back with the next renewal.

## On a new phone, or to change it

- **I already have a subscription** finds it again on your Google account, for instance on a new
  phone: **✓ Your subscription was found on this Google account.** The phone must use the Google
  account the subscription was taken with.
- **Manage the subscription (Google Play)** opens Google Play's page, to change the plan or cancel
  it.
- **Licence key**: if Notosaurus gave you a key (`nts_…`), paste it there and tap **Check**.

## Payment pending

When Google Play couldn't renew the subscription (an expired card, for instance), the app says
**Payment pending**: update your payment method in Google Play (**Manage the subscription**).
Once Google Play has received it, the subscription is active again: open the settings again.

What the service keeps about your subscription: see the [privacy policy](../app-privacy/).
