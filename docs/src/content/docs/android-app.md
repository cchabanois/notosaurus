The **Notosaurus app for Android** makes the cards on the phone itself: photograph the lesson,
check the cards, and they go straight into **AnkiDroid**. No computer, no AI key to get. It is
coming to Google Play.

It is the same Notosaurus as on the computer: the same page, instructions, card types, review,
children and tests to prepare. See [Photos and PDFs](../photos/), [Instructions](../instructions/)
and [Reviewing the cards](../review/): everything there works in the app too.

## Before you start

At the first launch, the app says in a few lines what it is and where the lessons go, then asks
you to accept its [privacy policy](../app-privacy/):

- Notosaurus is a **tool for parents**: you prepare the cards, your child revises them in Anki.
- It uses **Google's Gemini AI**. The photos, instructions and dictations go through the
  Notosaurus service while the cards are made, without being stored there, and are processed
  in the European Union. Your lessons stay on your phone.
- A notebook page often shows the child's name: hide it with **🖍️** before sending (see
  [Hiding a name or a school](../photos/#hiding-a-name-or-a-school)).

## Two ways to work

In the app's settings, **📍 Where does Notosaurus work?** chooses:

- **📱 On this phone** (the default): lessons on the phone, cards into AnkiDroid. With the
  subscription.
- **💻 On my computer**: with the [Anki add-on](../getting-started/), lessons and cards stay on
  the computer. Free.

### On this phone

The Notosaurus service does what needs an AI: reading the photos, making and correcting the
cards, explaining one, reading the backs aloud, drawing a picture or finding a free one
(Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and Pixabay). The lessons are kept in the app's private storage:
**🗂️ Lessons on this phone**, in the settings, says how many there are and their size, and
**Delete all the lessons** removes them (the cards already in AnkiDroid stay, with their sound).

### On my computer

With Notosaurus on the computer (Anki open with the add-on, the phone on the same Wi-Fi), the app
shows the computer's page: the lessons, the AI and the cards are the computer's, with your own AI
key, as in the phone's browser (see [On the phone](../phone/)).

1. On the computer: **Anki → Tools → Notosaurus → Open on the phone**.
2. In the app: **📍 Where does Notosaurus work?** → **💻 On my computer** → **Scan the QR code**.
   Google's code scanner opens: the app doesn't ask for the camera. Or **Type its address
   instead**, the one under the QR code.

In this mode, the AI's instructions and the other settings are on the computer:
**Anki → Tools → Notosaurus → Settings**. **Change computer** scans another one.

If the computer doesn't answer, the app says **💻 Your computer doesn't answer**: Notosaurus must
be running on it, and the phone on the same Wi-Fi. **Try again**, **Scan the QR code again**, or
**Use without computer**.

Each mode keeps its own lessons. Once a computer is connected, a long press on the app's icon
offers **Without computer** and **With my computer**, to open one or the other.

## The subscription

On this phone, the app works with a subscription, **by the month or by the year**, paid through
Google Play. The prices are shown in the app, as Google Play gives them.

- **The free trial starts with your first lesson**: no card to give, once per phone. Google checks
  that it is the app installed from Google Play on a real phone, and the app knows this phone by a
  fingerprint (never its identifier itself).
- **Credits**: the subscription gives credits each month. **🔑 Subscription**, in the settings,
  shows how many are left this month and today, and when they are renewed. What the service does
  for you uses them (the cards, the corrections, the voices, the pictures); before drawing a
  picture, the app says what it will cost.
- **I already have a subscription** finds it again on your Google account, for instance on a new
  phone. **Manage the subscription (Google Play)** changes or cancels it.
- **Payment pending**: when Google Play couldn't renew it, update your payment method in Google
  Play (**Manage the subscription**).

Without a subscription, the app is free with the Anki add-on on your computer: choose
**💻 On my computer**.

## AnkiDroid

The cards go into **AnkiDroid**, Anki's free app for Android. The first time, the app asks for
AnkiDroid's permission to add cards (AnkiDroid's own dialog); if AnkiDroid isn't installed, it
opens its Google Play page. Open AnkiDroid once after installing it, so that it creates its
collection. **📥 AnkiDroid**, in the settings, says where things stand.

- **Add to Anki** writes the cards into AnkiDroid, with their sounds and pictures. Sent again, a
  lesson updates its cards instead of duplicating them. AnkiDroid syncs them with AnkiWeb as
  usual: see [Sending to Anki](../send-to-anki/#in-the-app).
- **Open AnkiDroid**, at the top of the page and at its foot, opens it in one tap.
- **✎ Notosaurus**, under every answer in AnkiDroid, opens the card in the app to correct it
  (see [The cards in Anki](../in-anki/#the-cards-in-anki)).
- A test to prepare opens its cards in AnkiDroid, to make its filtered deck in three taps (see
  [Tests to prepare](../lessons/#tests-to-prepare)).

## The app's own

- **🎤 Dictate**: say an instruction or a correction instead of typing it. The app asks for the
  microphone the first time.
- **⚑ Report**, on a card, reports to Notosaurus a wrong or unsuitable content the AI made (see
  [Reporting a content](../review/#reporting-a-content)).
- **The voices** are Google's, through the Notosaurus service: [the voice settings](../voices/)
  are the same.
- **No `.apkg`**: the cards go straight into AnkiDroid.
- **Updates** come through Google Play. When a new version is available, the app says so, with an
  **Update** button.

## The settings

**⚙️** opens the app's own settings:

- **🔑 Subscription**: the plan, the credits, the subscription.
- **📥 AnkiDroid**: installed, allowed.
- **📍 Where does Notosaurus work?**: on this phone, or on your computer.
- **📝 Instructions for the AI**: instructions for every child, and the helps on the back. Each
  child's own are in their card (**🧒** on the main page).
- **Card voices**: the speed, and a voice per language.
- **🗂️ Lessons on this phone**.
- **ℹ️ About**: the version, the licence and the libraries' licences.

What the app sends and keeps: see the [app's privacy policy](../app-privacy/).
