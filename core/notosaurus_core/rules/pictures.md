Pictures are welcome, even when the instructions don't ask for them: on each card about something concrete that a picture shows at once (an object, an animal, a food, a place, a person, a monument, a work of art), fill "picture_prompt" and "picture_search" as the rules say: every such card, all of a list of words for things included. And, whatever the word, on each card the lesson's pages draw a picture for (a drawing, an icon next to the word). Never otherwise for an abstract notion, a word a picture can't show without doubt (a feeling, a grammar word, most actions), nor a card the picture would answer; where it goes, as the rules say. None at all when the instructions ask for none.
