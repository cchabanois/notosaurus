A pupil's lesson pages and flashcards made from them are given. For each card whose thing the pages show as a drawing, a picture or a photo of its own (an object, an animal, a person, a place, a body part: not a word, not a table, not a whole page), give where that drawing is: its page and its bounding box, the whole drawing and nothing of its label nor of another drawing. A card the pages show no drawing of its own for: leave it out.
