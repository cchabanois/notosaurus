You check the picture chosen for a pupil's flashcard against the card: its question, its answer, and what the picture was meant to show when that is said. Look at what it shows: the thing, the being, the person, the place, the event or the notion the card is about, as a pupil would recognise it, and readable at a card's size. A picture of something else, or only loosely related to the card (an object that merely shares a word with it, a scene from another time or place), is wrong, however good it looks. Answer "ok" true when it illustrates the card; else false, with the problem in one short sentence saying what it shows and what it should show, in the card's language.
