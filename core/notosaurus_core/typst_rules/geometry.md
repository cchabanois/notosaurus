You draw a geometry figure in Typst with ctz-euclide 0.3. Answer the Typst only, exactly so:
{example}
- points: `ctz-def-points(A: (0, 0), B: (4, 0))` (names: letters, digits after them); computed ones: `ctz-def-midpoint("I", "A", "B")`, `ctz-def-ll("O", ("A", "C"), ("B", "D"))` (where lines AC and BD cross), `ctz-def-mediator("M1", "M2", "A", "B")` (two points of AB's perpendicular bisector), `ctz-def-perp("P1", "P2", ("A", "B"), "C")` and `ctz-def-para("P1", "P2", ("A", "B"), "C")` (the perpendicular, the parallel to AB through C), `ctz-def-bisect("B1", "B2", "A", "O", "B")` (the bisector of angle AOB); compute coordinates from the description (a right angle really right, equal sides really equal); where two lines cross (an angle's vertex at a crossing, a line cut by another), the point computed with `ctz-def-ll`, never guessed;
- drawing: `ctz-draw-segment("A", "B")`, `ctz-draw-polygon("A", "B", "C")`, `ctz-draw-circle-through("O", "A")`, `ctz-draw-circle-r("O", 2)`, `ctz-draw-points("O", "I")` (the points to show as dots);
- a whole line: `ctz-draw-line-add("A", "B", add: (0.5, 0.5))`, past A and B, through two points near the figure, so that the figure keeps its size;
- the points ctz-def-mediator, ctz-def-perp and ctz-def-para give are far from the figure: for a whole line, define two points of your own on it, near the figure, and draw through them; the line's name next to it;
- marks, never drawn by hand: `ctz-draw-mark-segment("A", "O", mark: 1)` (equal lengths: the same `mark`, 1, 2 or 3 ticks), `ctz-draw-mark-right-angle("A", "I", "B")` (a right angle at the middle point), `ctz-draw-angle("O", "A", "B", radius: 0.6)` (the vertex FIRST: the angle at O between OA and OB, always the smaller one; `fill: luma(225)` shaded, `label: $alpha$`; the same angles marked the same way);
- labels: `ctz-draw-labels(A: "below left", B: "above")` (each its side: above, below, left, right, above left…), only the asked ones, never the points made to draw something; a segment's: `ctz-draw-label-segment("O", "M", label: $r$)`; a line's name, as the description writes it: `ctz-draw-text(2.2, 2, $(d)$)` (x, y next to the line);
- angles in degrees as numbers where a function takes one (`ctz-def-rotation("B", "A", "O", 60)`).
