You draw a solid in perspective, or a coordinate frame of space, in Typst with the functions given (cetz): they place its vertices, its outline and its hidden edges themselves (dashed). Answer the Typst only, exactly so:
{example}
- `cuboid(length, width, height)`: a cuboid, a cube with three equal lengths (`cuboid(3, 3, 3)`); its vertices `names: ("A", "B", "C", "D", "E", "F", "G", "H")`: ABCD its base (A front left, B front right, C back right, D back left), EFGH above A, B, C, D; its lengths written on its edges with `length-label:` (AB), `width-label:` (BC), `height-label:` (AE);
- `prism(base, height)`: a right prism, its base the polygon of `(x, y)` points (y going away from the viewer: `((0, 0), (4, 0), (1, 3))` a triangle), standing on it; `lying: true` for a prism lying on a side face, its base then upright, `(x, z)` points (`prism(((0, 0), (4, 0), (2, 3)), 6, lying: true)`); `names:` the base's vertices then the top's in the same order; `height-label:`;
- `pyramid(side: 4, height: 5)`: a pyramid on a square (`depth: 3` a rectangle), or on any polygon `base: ((0, 0), (4, 0), (1.5, 3))` (a tetrahedron), its apex above the base's centre; `names:` the base's vertices (as the cuboid's: front left first), then the apex's (`("A", "B", "C", "D", "S")`); `foot: "O"` names the base's centre and draws the height to it with its right angle, `height-label: $h$`; `side-label:` on the first side;
- `cylinder(radius, height)`: standing on a base, `lying: true` its axis horizontal (`height` then its length); `radius-label:`, `height-label:`; `centre-names: ("O", "O'")` names its bases' centres (below, above) and draws its axis;
- `cone(radius, height)`: `apex-name: "S"`, `centre-name: "O"` (the base's centre, the height drawn to it with its right angle), `base-name: "A"` (a point of the base's circle, at the end of the slant height on the right); `radius-label:`, `height-label:`, `slant-label:` (on that slant height);
- `sphere(radius)`: with its equator; `centre-name: "O"`, `radius-label:`, `diameter: ("A", "B")` a diameter drawn through the centre with its ends named; `hemisphere(radius)`: a half-ball on its flat face, `centre-name:`, `radius-label:`;
- `crystal(lattice: "simple", cells: 3, species: ((name: [A#super[+]], fill: blue, size: 1), (name: [B#super[−]], fill: orange, size: 0.6)))`: a crystal, balls (atoms, ions) on a cubic lattice in perspective, every ball's place and species, the drawing order (a ball in front hides those behind it), the lattice's lines and the legend computed: `lattice:` `"simple"` (a ball at each node of the cubes), `"body-centred"` (and one at each cube's centre) or `"face-centred"` (and one at each face's centre); `cells:` the cubes along each edge, one fewer than the balls along it (a block of n × n × n balls: `cells:` n − 1; a unit cell: `cells: 1`); `species:` one or two, each its `name:` (the legend: a symbol's letters as text, its charge `#super[…]`; in `$…$` a symbol of several letters quoted, `$"Ab"^+$`), `fill:` its colour, `size:` its ball's size relative to the other's: on a simple lattice two species alternate along every line (an ionic crystal), on a centred one the second is at the centres or the faces' centres; `edge-label:` the cube's edge, a double arrow below it; `compact: true` the balls touching (the compact model), `cut: true` each ball cut by the cube's faces (only what lies inside it); never a ball, a line or a legend placed yourself;
- lengths as the description gives them (only their ratio shows: the solid is drawn about 4 cm, a cube's edges equal), or in proportion when it gives none; a length written as the description writes it, `[3 cm]`; labels in `$…$`, only those it asks for, names only when it names the vertices; never a vertex, an edge or a dash placed yourself, never a coordinate in 3D for a solid;
- several solids to compare at the same scale: each with `largest:` the greatest length among them all, side by side: `#grid(columns: 2, align: bottom, column-gutter: 1cm, cylinder(1, 5, largest: 6), cylinder(3, 1, largest: 6))`;
- a section by a plane: the solid's own function with `section:`, its place computed (the section filled grey, its hidden sides dashed), never a plane, a line or a point drawn yourself, never the solid without it: on a cuboid, a prism or a pyramid, `section: 2` a plane parallel to the base, that far from it (in the solid's lengths), or `section: (parallel: "ABFE", at: 1.5)` parallel to a face (its vertices' names, the names given; or `"base"`, `"top"`, `"front"`, `"back"`, `"left"`, `"right"`), `section-names: ("A'", "B'", "C'", "D'")` its vertices, one per vertex of that face in the same order (each on the edge from it); on a cylinder or a cone, `section: 2` parallel to the base, that far from it, `section-centre: "O'"` its centre; on a sphere, `section: 3` that far from the centre (below it: negative), `section-centre: "H"` (with [OH] and its right angle), `section-label:` the circle's radius [HA], `distance-label:` OH, `radius-label:` then on [OA], A on the circle;
- a coordinate frame of space (O ; i, j, k) and no solid, points given by their coordinates (a line's or a plane's equation, a parametric representation, vectors in space): `space(points: (A: (2, 0, 0), B: (0, 3, 0), C: (0, 0, 1)), planes: (("A", "B", "C"),))`: the axes, their unit vectors, each point placed from its coordinates (x towards the viewer, y to the right, z up), each name set, what a plane hides dashed, all computed; `points:` every point by its name and (x, y, z), as the description gives them (a point of a line or a plane computed from its equation or its parameter beforehand, its coordinates written as numbers); `segments: (("A", "B"),)`, `lines: (("A", "B"),)` the whole line through two of its points, `planes: (("A", "B", "C"),)` a plane by three or more of its points (where it meets the axes, when the description gives them), `vectors: (("A", "B", $arrow(u)$),)` a vector from a point to another, its name optional; `guides: ("M",)` a point's dotted lines down to the plane (xOy) and to the axes, only when the description reads its coordinates; `units: none` without the unit vectors, `axes: none` without x, y, z; only the points, lines and planes the description draws; a solid in a frame (a cube ABCDEFGH and the frame (A ; AB, AD, AE)), points on it or lines through it: `space` too, each vertex by its coordinates in that frame and `faces:` each face by its vertices' names (`faces: (("A", "B", "C", "D"), ("E", "F", "G", "H"), ("A", "B", "F", "E"), ("B", "C", "G", "F"), ("C", "D", "H", "G"), ("D", "A", "E", "H"))`), its hidden edges and what it hides computed, `origin: none, axes: none, units: none` when the frame is on a vertex (its vectors then `vectors: (("A", "B"),)`); a solid without points or lines of its own: its own function above;
- a net, or a section by a plane none of these give (slanted, along a cylinder's axis): none of these functions.
