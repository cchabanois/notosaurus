### Added

A lesson there already is kept, or replaced when the file's is newer; its cards already in Anki are found again, never added twice. A lesson from the phone keeps its sounds and gets a voice of the computer. For developers: `app/transfer.py` (the format, versioned: `manifest.json`, `lessons/<id>/`, `children.json`, `exams.json`, `prompts.json`), `/api/admin/transfer` (GET: the zip; POST: take one in).

### Changed

- A photo is kept as taken, no longer turned upright by guess: the guess (from the direction of a line of text the AI named) was right only now and then, and sometimes turned a sideways page upside down, its masks with it. **Turn** (↻) puts a page upright, its hidden parts and frame with it. For developers: the AI isn't asked for a line of text any more (`TextLine`, `diagrams.turns`, `straighten` gone); `/v1/extract`'s `turns` are all 0 (kept for the 1.1.0 apps).
- A picture is any card's in Anki, no longer a card of its own: a dictation with its picture is still a dictation (heard on its question, typed), a reversed or typed card keeps its picture, on the question or with the answer. Anki asks for a full sync once, the note types getting their picture's fields. Picture cards sent by 1.0.0 are sent again as new notes (the old ones offered for deletion: their review history isn't kept). For developers: every text note type has `Picture`, `BackPicture`, `Id` and `Source`, after its other fields; the "Notosaurus image" note types are gone; a note's key is its card's id when it has a picture, its front otherwise (`note_types.key_of`, Android's `NoteType.keyOf`: a card without a picture keeps its GUID); AnkiDroid keeps as it is a note type lacking fields.
- The card's picture field no longer tells the AI "only when the instructions ask for a picture" while the "Pictures on cards" setting says "even when they don't": it now refers to the rules and the request. Measured twice each: with the setting on, school things 12/12 and school subjects 11/11 pictured; off, none; irregular verbs none either way.
- For developers: Notosaurus's prompts' texts in files of their own, `static/prompts/<lang>/<key>.md` (a line per line, no escaped \n); their names and options stay in the i18n files; the computer and the phone read them, the texts unchanged.
- For developers: the fixed rules given to the AI in files of their own, `core/notosaurus_core/rules/<name>.md` (the system prompt, safety, pictures, helps, explanation, dictation…), a paragraph or a bullet per line; what each call adds stays in `prompts.py`'s functions. The text the AI is given unchanged.

### Security

- Android: a release always talks to the real relay. The relay's address (the settings' "Advanced", in About) is shown and taken in a debug or staging build only; one saved before is ignored, so a typo or someone's "tip" can't send the lessons elsewhere.
- The address check also refuses a Host ending with a line break (Python's "$" let it through).
- Notosaurus answers only when opened by an IP address, `localhost` or the address in `NOTOSAURUS_PUBLIC_URL`: a website open on the computer can no longer reach it by pointing its own name at the computer (DNS rebinding) to read the lessons, delete them or change the settings.
- A new settings password needs 8 characters at least (the one already chosen still works), and after 5 wrong passwords the device trying them must wait, longer at each new try (up to 15 minutes).
- A card's id is checked (letters and digits only, any other replaced by a fresh one): it names its picture's file, and could have written elsewhere on Windows.
- A photo heavier than 4 MB is refused (“A photo is too heavy”) instead of being read whole into memory.
- Nothing of a lesson in the logs any more: a picture that fails, a free picture not found, a sound not made or a figure left undescribed logged its subject or text (or an error quoting it), on the computer and on the relay. They now say only what went wrong (an error code, a count, a model).
- The Docker image runs Notosaurus as its own user, not root. A `notosaurus-data` volume made by an older image is handed over once with `docker run --rm -u 0 -v notosaurus-data:/data notosaurus chown -R notosaurus: /data`.
- Wrong settings passwords never lock out the computer itself, and those typed on it don't count: a neighbour on the Wi-Fi guessing, or a shared address (Docker, Tailscale), could keep the parent out of the settings.
- A picture or photo claiming an enormous size in a few kilobytes (a “decompression bomb”) is refused as a photo Notosaurus can't read, before anything is decoded: it made an error (and, on the relay, “service unavailable”), or could fill the memory. Lesson photos, your own picture for a card, the free pictures found, on the computer and on the relay.
- A `Host` header with a port that can't be (over 65535, or 0) or brackets around no IPv6 address is refused like any unknown name; the brackets made an error.
